# Charizard

Builds include physical setup with Mega Charizard X and special sun offense with Mega Charizard Y; condition on the Mega Stone.

## Identity and form

Types: [Fire](../types/fire.md), [Flying](../types/flying.md). Weight: 90.5 kg.

Battle forms: [Charizard-Mega-X](../pokemon/charizardmegax.md), [Charizard-Mega-Y](../pokemon/charizardmegay.md).

Intrinsic abilities: [Blaze](../abilities/blaze.md), [Solar Power](../abilities/solarpower.md). Use the revealed ability when known.
