# Metagross-Mega

Physical Steel/Psychic attacker with a Mega Evolution option.

## Identity and form

Types: [Steel](../types/steel.md), [Psychic](../types/psychic.md). Weight: 942.9 kg.

Battle form reached from [Metagross](../pokemon/metagross.md). Base stats and intrinsic abilities below describe **Metagross-Mega**.

Transformation item: [Metagrossite](../items/metagrossite.md).

Intrinsic abilities: [Tough Claws](../abilities/toughclaws.md). Use the revealed ability when known.
