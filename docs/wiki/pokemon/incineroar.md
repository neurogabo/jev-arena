## Tactical cues

- Fake Out is available only on its first turn after entering; a successful switch out and return restores that opportunity.
- Intimidate or Parting Shot can activate an opposing Defiant or a held White Herb; check the opposing ability and item.
- Ghost typing prevents Fake Out damage; protection, relevant priority-blocking effects, or flinch immunity can deny its disruption.
