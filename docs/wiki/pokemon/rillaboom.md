# Rillaboom

Grassy Terrain setter with Fake Out, priority Grassy Glide, and physical pressure.

## Identity and form

Types: [Grass](../types/grass.md). Weight: 90 kg.

Intrinsic abilities: [Overgrow](../abilities/overgrow.md), [Grassy Surge](../abilities/grassysurge.md). Use the revealed ability when known.

## Tactical cues

- Grassy Surge replaces other terrain on entry. Grassy Glide gains priority in Grassy Terrain.
- Fake Out and priority Grassy Glide are vulnerable to opposing priority-blocking effects.
