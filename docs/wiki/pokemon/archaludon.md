# Archaludon

Bulky special attacker; rain enables immediate Electro Shot and Stamina supports Body Press.

## Identity and form

Types: [Steel](../types/steel.md), [Dragon](../types/dragon.md). Weight: 60 kg.

Intrinsic abilities: [Stamina](../abilities/stamina.md), [Sturdy](../abilities/sturdy.md), [Stalwart](../abilities/stalwart.md). Use the revealed ability when known.
