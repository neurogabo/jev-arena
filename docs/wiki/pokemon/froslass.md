# Froslass

Snow-based special pressure with a Mega Evolution option.

## Identity and form

Types: [Ice](../types/ice.md), [Ghost](../types/ghost.md). Weight: 26.6 kg.

Battle forms: [Froslass-Mega](../pokemon/froslassmega.md).

Intrinsic abilities: [Snow Cloak](../abilities/snowcloak.md), [Cursed Body](../abilities/cursedbody.md). Use the revealed ability when known.
