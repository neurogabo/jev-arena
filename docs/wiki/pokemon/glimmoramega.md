# Glimmora-Mega

Special Rock/Poison attacker with Adaptability; Toxic Debris belongs to its starting form.

## Identity and form

Types: [Rock](../types/rock.md), [Poison](../types/poison.md). Weight: 77 kg.

Battle form reached from [Glimmora](../pokemon/glimmora.md). Base stats and intrinsic abilities below describe **Glimmora-Mega**.

Transformation item: [Glimmoranite](../items/glimmoranite.md).

Intrinsic abilities: [Adaptability](../abilities/adaptability.md). Use the revealed ability when known.
