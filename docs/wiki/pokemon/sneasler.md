# Sneasler

Fast Fighting/Poison attacker with Fake Out or Unburden plans.

## Identity and form

Types: [Fighting](../types/fighting.md), [Poison](../types/poison.md). Weight: 43 kg.

Intrinsic abilities: [Pressure](../abilities/pressure.md), [Unburden](../abilities/unburden.md), [Poison Touch](../abilities/poisontouch.md). Use the revealed ability when known.

## Tactical cues

- Consuming White Herb or a terrain seed can activate Unburden. Merely revealing an unconsumed item does not grant the Speed boost.
- Priority disruption can still act before a faster Unburden attacker.
