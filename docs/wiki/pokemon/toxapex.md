# Toxapex

Defensive support using chip damage, Wide Guard, and Regenerator cycling.

## Identity and form

Types: [Poison](../types/poison.md), [Water](../types/water.md). Weight: 14.5 kg.

Intrinsic abilities: [Merciless](../abilities/merciless.md), [Limber](../abilities/limber.md), [Regenerator](../abilities/regenerator.md). Use the revealed ability when known.

## Tactical cues

- Infestation and Toxic create continuing damage; Wide Guard protects its side from eligible spread moves.
- Regenerator restores HP when switching out.
