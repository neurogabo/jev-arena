# Garchomp

Builds include physical Dragon/Ground offense and fast special offense after evolving into Mega Garchomp Z; condition on the item and transformation.

## Identity and form

Types: [Dragon](../types/dragon.md), [Ground](../types/ground.md). Weight: 95 kg.

Battle forms: [Garchomp-Mega](../pokemon/garchompmega.md), [Garchomp-Mega-Z](../pokemon/garchompmegaz.md).

Intrinsic abilities: [Sand Veil](../abilities/sandveil.md), [Rough Skin](../abilities/roughskin.md). Use the revealed ability when known.
