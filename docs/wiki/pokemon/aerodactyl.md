## Tactical cues

- Rock Slide pressures both foes; its flinch threat matters only against targets that have not acted.
- Dual Wingbeat threatens Flying-weak targets and can defeat Focus Sash in two hits. Ice Fang covers Ice-weak targets.
- Tough Claws boosts contact attacks such as Dual Wingbeat and Ice Fang, not Rock Slide. Mega Evolution replaces Unnerve.
- Fake Out, opposing speed control, Wide Guard, and priority attacks can disrupt its plan.
