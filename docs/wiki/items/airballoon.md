# Air Balloon

`item:airballoon` · Holder is immune to Ground-type attacks. Pops when holder is hit.

## Definition

Holder is immune to Ground-type attacks. Pops when holder is hit.

Apply the effect only while its holder/item state satisfies the rule. Revealed, consumed, removed, exchanged, and suppressed are different states.
