# Leftovers

`item:leftovers` · At the end of every turn, holder restores 1/16 of its max HP.

## Definition

At the end of every turn, holder restores 1/16 of its max HP.

Apply the effect only while its holder/item state satisfies the rule. Revealed, consumed, removed, exchanged, and suppressed are different states.
