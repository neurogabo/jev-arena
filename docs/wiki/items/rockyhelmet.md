# Rocky Helmet

`item:rockyhelmet` · If holder is hit by a contact move, the attacker loses 1/6 of its max HP.

## Definition

If holder is hit by a contact move, the attacker loses 1/6 of its max HP.

Apply the effect only while its holder/item state satisfies the rule. Revealed, consumed, removed, exchanged, and suppressed are different states.
