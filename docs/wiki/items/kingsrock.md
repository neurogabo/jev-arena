# King's Rock

`item:kingsrock` · Holder's attacks without a chance to flinch gain a 10% chance to flinch.

## Definition

Holder's attacks without a chance to make the target flinch gain a 10% chance to make the target flinch. Evolves Poliwhirl into Politoed and Slowpoke into Slowking when traded.

Apply the effect only while its holder/item state satisfies the rule. Revealed, consumed, removed, exchanged, and suppressed are different states.
