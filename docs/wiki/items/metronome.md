# Metronome

`item:metronome` · Damage of moves used on consecutive turns is increased. Max 2× after 5 turns.

## Definition

Damage of moves used on consecutive turns is increased. Max 2× after 5 turns.

Apply the effect only while its holder/item state satisfies the rule. Revealed, consumed, removed, exchanged, and suppressed are different states.
