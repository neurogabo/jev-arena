# Expert Belt

`item:expertbelt` · Holder's attacks that are super effective against the target do 1.2× damage.

## Definition

Holder's attacks that are super effective against the target do 1.2× damage.

Apply the effect only while its holder/item state satisfies the rule. Revealed, consumed, removed, exchanged, and suppressed are different states.
