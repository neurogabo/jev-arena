# Light Ball

`item:lightball` · If held by a Pikachu, its Attack and Sp. Atk are doubled.

## Definition

If held by a Pikachu, its Attack and Sp. Atk are doubled.

Apply the effect only while its holder/item state satisfies the rule. Revealed, consumed, removed, exchanged, and suppressed are different states.
