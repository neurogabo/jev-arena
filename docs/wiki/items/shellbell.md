# Shell Bell

`item:shellbell` · After an attack, holder gains 1/8 of the damage in HP dealt to other Pokemon.

## Definition

After an attack, holder gains 1/8 of the damage in HP dealt to other Pokemon.

Apply the effect only while its holder/item state satisfies the rule. Revealed, consumed, removed, exchanged, and suppressed are different states.
