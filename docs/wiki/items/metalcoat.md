# Metal Coat

`item:metalcoat` · Holder's Steel-type attacks have 1.2× power.

## Definition

Holder's Steel-type attacks have 1.2× power. Evolves Onix into Steelix and Scyther into Scizor when traded.

Apply the effect only while its holder/item state satisfies the rule. Revealed, consumed, removed, exchanged, and suppressed are different states.
