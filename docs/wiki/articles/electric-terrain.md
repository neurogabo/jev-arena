# Electric Terrain

Grounded Electric attack boost, sleep prevention, Yawn interaction, and existing sleep.

## Reference text

Dependencies: M16, grounding, sleep and Yawn.

Electric attacks by grounded Pokémon have their power multiplied by 1.3. Grounded Pokémon cannot newly fall asleep, including through Rest, while the prevention applies. Already sleeping Pokémon are not automatically awakened.

Grounded Pokémon cannot acquire Yawn's delayed effect or fall asleep from an existing Yawn effect while the relevant Electric Terrain protection applies. A Flying or otherwise ungrounded Pokémon does not automatically receive this sleep protection.
