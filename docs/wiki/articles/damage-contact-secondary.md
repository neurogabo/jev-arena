# Damage, contact, and secondary effects

Distinct hit, damage, contact, stat-change, secondary-effect, and multi-hit triggers.

## Reference text

Contact-triggered effects depend on actual contact and the applicable ability/item rules. Rocky Helmet, Rough Skin, and protection variants can punish contact. A contact-preventing effect may alter those interactions without changing the move from physical to special or vice versa.

Taking damage, being hit, making contact, having a stat lowered, crossing an HP threshold, and being knocked out are different triggers. An effect tied to one of them should not be generalized to all the others.

A secondary effect is attempted only when its own conditions are reached. A knockout, Substitute, immunity, Shield Dust, Sheer Force, or another relevant effect can change whether the secondary effect occurs. Separate a move's secondary effect on the target from its guaranteed drawback on the user.

Multi-hit attacks can trigger effects between hits. A Substitute or Focus Sash can stop one hit without protecting against later hits. HP recovery or reactive effects can occur during the sequence. A move with two hits is not necessarily equivalent to one hit with twice its listed power.
