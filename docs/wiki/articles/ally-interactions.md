# Helping Hand and ally interaction

Helping Hand, beneficial or harmful ally attacks, Telepathy, Friend Guard, and Ally Switch.

## Reference text

Include when ally support, an ally-targeted attack, or ally damage is relevant. Dependencies: targeting, move timing, relevant ability and protection rules.

Helping Hand normally has priority +5 and multiplies the power of the partner's applicable attack that turn by 1.5. It fails if there is no eligible partner or the partner has already moved. It does not grant the partner another action. The partner still needs to execute an attack that can benefit, reach its target, and pass the relevant checks.

A support move can affect both sides' Pokémon if its targeting permits that. An ally-targeted damaging move can activate an immunity ability or another beneficial reaction, but it can also cause genuine damage or trigger harmful effects. Do not assume “ally” automatically makes an attack harmless.

Telepathy prevents damage from an ally's damaging attacks when effective. Friend Guard reduces eligible damage received by the holder's allies to 3/4; it does not provide that reduction to the holder itself. Each ability's suppression and bypass rules still apply.

Ally Switch swaps the two allied active positions without switching either Pokémon to the bench. It does not reset ordinary switch-out counters or activate ordinary entry abilities. Repeated use has its own diminishing success mechanic and should not be treated as indefinitely guaranteed.
