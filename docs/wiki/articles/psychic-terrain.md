# Psychic Terrain

Grounded Psychic power, opposing priority protection, ally exceptions, and Expanding Force changes.

## Reference text

Dependencies: M16, grounding, priority, targeting.

Psychic attacks by grounded Pokémon have their power multiplied by 1.3. Grounded targets are protected from opposing moves with priority greater than zero when those moves attempt to affect them.

The target's grounding matters for this protection. An airborne attacker does not bypass it merely by being airborne, and an airborne target is not protected merely because its partner is grounded. Ally-directed moves are exempt from this opposing-move protection; Psychic Terrain does not block Helping Hand merely for having positive priority.

Expanding Force gains its special power increase and all-foes targeting when its user is grounded in Psychic Terrain. That change can make spread-damage and Wide Guard rules relevant. A grounded-target priority block is different from the broad side protection supplied by Armor Tail.
