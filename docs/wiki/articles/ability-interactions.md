# Ability interactions

Ability-effect families, triggers, prevention, suppression, bypass, and changed abilities; exact entity exceptions still needed.

## Reference text

Include only entries matching a known ability or explicit ability hypothesis, together with the underlying mechanic. These summaries describe common interactions; an entity's complete resolved definition remains necessary for its unusual exceptions.

| Ability or family | Decision-relevant rule |
| --- | --- |
| Intimidate | On entry, attempts to lower opposing active Pokémon's Attack by one stage. Substitute, prevention abilities, and specific Intimidate immunities can block it. |
| Inner Focus | Prevents flinching and the ordinary Intimidate Attack reduction while effective. It does not make an incoming Fake Out deal zero damage. |
| Clear Body / Hyper Cutter | Clear Body prevents externally caused stat-stage reductions; Hyper Cutter protects Attack specifically. Self-inflicted drops are a different case. |
| Defiant / Competitive | React to applicable stat reductions caused by an opponent with +2 Attack or +2 Special Attack, respectively. Do not assume a partner's reduction triggers them. |
| Contrary / Simple | Contrary reverses stage-change directions. Simple doubles the stage-change amount. Stage caps still apply. |
| Unaware | Ignores specified opposing offensive/accuracy stages when defending and defensive/evasion stages when attacking. It does not erase those stages or ignore every item, ability, weather effect, or burn penalty. |
| Prankster | Adds +1 priority to status moves. Applicable opposing Dark targets are immune; self/ally support and field effects need their own targeting interpretation. |
| Armor Tail / Dazzling / Queenly Majesty | Prevent eligible opposing positive-priority moves from affecting the holder or its allies. They do not require their protected recipients to be grounded. |
| Levitate / Eelevate | Provide the applicable Ground and grounding-related immunities while effective. Eelevate also has a knockout-triggered stat increase; that extra rule must be supplied if the ability is present. |
| Lightning Rod / Storm Drain | Provide the relevant Electric/Water immunity, redirection, and +1 Special Attack reaction. See M13. |
| Water Absorb / Volt Absorb / Earth Eater | An applicable Water/Electric/Ground move is negated and can restore one quarter maximum HP. Full HP does not turn the immunity off. |
| Sap Sipper | Negates applicable Grass moves and can raise Attack one stage. |
| Flash Fire | Negates applicable Fire moves and establishes its Fire-offense boost after activation. Blocking a Fire hit can also prevent it from thawing the holder. |
| Guts / Quick Feet | Status-dependent Attack or Speed boosts, with their respective burn/paralysis modifier exceptions. They do not cure the status. |
| Natural Cure / Regenerator | On ordinary eligible switch-out, cure major status or recover one third maximum HP, respectively. Champions explicitly reports these effects in its protocol. |
| Run Away — Champions | Removes the ordinary trapping restriction through its trapping hooks. It does not imply unrestricted choice during every recharge or move lock. |
| Healer — Champions | At its residual opportunity, independently has a 50% chance to cure an adjacent ally's existing major status. It does not cure the holder through this ally effect. |
| Sturdy | At full HP, survives an otherwise lethal eligible hit with 1 HP; blocks one-hit knockout moves. A later hit can still knock it out. |
| Multiscale | Halves eligible attack damage while the holder is at full HP. Entry damage can remove that condition before an attack. |
| Rough Skin / Iron Barbs | An applicable contact attacker loses one eighth of its own maximum HP. |
| Magic Guard | Prevents many indirect damage sources, without eliminating status, move restrictions, HP costs, confusion self-damage, or every recoil-like effect. |
| Shield Dust | Prevents applicable secondary effects from other Pokémon's attacks. An effect can be secondary even with a 100% listed chance. |
| Sheer Force | Boosts qualifying attacks by 1.3 and removes their qualifying secondary effects. It also suppresses specified after-move reactions; do not infer the full reaction list from the power boost alone. |
| Serene Grace | Increases qualifying secondary-effect chances under its rules; it does not guarantee that the primary hit succeeds. |
| Skill Link | Makes qualifying variable-hit moves use their maximum hit count and changes certain per-hit accuracy handling. |
| Soundproof / Bulletproof / Overcoat | Prevent their specified sound, bullet, or powder/weather interactions. A visual animation is not evidence that a move has a particular tag. |
| Insomnia / Sweet Veil | Prevent relevant sleep. Insomnia can cure its holder on gaining the ability; Sweet Veil's protection does not immediately wake already sleeping allies. |
| Purifying Salt | Prevents major status and Yawn and reduces incoming Ghost attack damage through its offensive-stat modifier. |
| Battle Armor / Shell Armor | Prevent critical hits while effective. |
| Pressure | Can increase opposing PP expenditure for applicable targeted moves. It does not simply double every opposing move's PP cost regardless of target. |
| Unnerve | Prevents opposing ordinary berry consumption while active. It does not delete the berry. |
| Scrappy | Allows applicable Normal and Fighting moves to affect Ghost targets and provides its Intimidate immunity. Other immunities and protections can still apply. |

Ability negation deserves its own distinction. **Mold Breaker-style bypass** applies to an enumerated set of abilities for relevant move interactions. **Neutralizing Gas** suppresses applicable other abilities while its active conditions hold. **Gastro Acid**, **Skill Swap**, and **Entrainment** have distinct suppression or replacement rules and lists of abilities that cannot be affected. Never turn any of these into “all abilities are always ignored.”

An ability can change during Mega Evolution, copying, swapping, suppression ending, or a form transition. Entry-like triggers on gaining an ability can therefore matter in the middle of a turn. Preserve current ability state and activation history where known.
