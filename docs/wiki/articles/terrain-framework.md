# Terrain framework

Terrain replacement, duration, grounding, side-independent effects, and identifying whose grounding matters.

## Reference text

Include with any terrain entry. Dependencies: grounding, duration, active ability/item state.

One terrain can be active at a time, independently of weather. A new different terrain replaces the previous terrain. Terrain normally lasts five turns including its setup turn, or eight if the setter has an effective Terrain Extender.

Many terrain effects require grounding, but they do not all test the same Pokémon. Type boosts ordinarily test the attacking Pokémon; priority protection and status prevention test the affected target. Terrain does not automatically change a Pokémon's type.

A Pokémon becoming grounded or ungrounded during the turn can gain or lose a terrain interaction. A terrain-setting switch or Mega Evolution can establish terrain before ordinary attacks. Terrain does not protect only the side that created it.
