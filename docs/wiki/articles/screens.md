# Reflect, Light Screen, and Aurora Veil

Reflect, Light Screen, and Aurora Veil: doubles reduction, duration, setup conditions, bypass, and removal.

## Reference text

Include when a screen exists or can be set. Dependencies: move category, critical hits, screen removal, Infiltrator if relevant.

Reflect reduces eligible physical damage; Light Screen reduces eligible special damage. In doubles, the pinned engine uses a damage multiplier of **2732/4096**, approximately two thirds. This is a doubles-format reduction even when only one Pokémon remains active on that side, rather than a rule based solely on counting present partners.

Aurora Veil reduces eligible damage of both categories but requires effective snow when set. Once established, it does not require snow to remain in order to persist. Its reduction does not stack again with Reflect or Light Screen for the same attack.

These screens normally last five turns including the setup turn, or eight when the setter has an effective Light Clay. Critical hits and applicable Infiltrator attacks bypass their reduction. Brick Break and Psychic Fangs can remove relevant screens before their damage is calculated; Defog also has screen-removal behavior under its own rules.

Screens do not mean that all HP loss is reduced. Fixed damage, residual damage, costs, and special damage-setting effects require their own definitions.
