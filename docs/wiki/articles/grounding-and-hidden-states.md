# Grounding and semi-invulnerability

Grounding, levitation, airborne effects, semi-invulnerability, and attacker versus recipient terrain checks.

## Reference text

Grounding determines exposure to many terrain benefits, terrain protections, entry hazards, Ground attacks, and trapping effects. It is not inferred from appearance.

Gravity, Ingrain, Smack Down, or an effective Iron Ball can ground a Pokémon. Otherwise, a current Flying type, an active applicable levitation ability such as Levitate or Eelevate, Magnet Rise, Telekinesis, or an effective Air Balloon can keep it ungrounded. Some moves or ability-negation effects override a particular immunity check.

Grounding can change during the turn. A type change, balloon being popped, ability suppression, or Gravity can therefore change whether a later effect works.

Semi-invulnerability is separate. A Pokémon using the hidden phase of Fly, Bounce, Dig, Dive, or Phantom Force is not simply “ungrounded.” Different attacks can reach different hidden states. A grounded Pokémon can still be temporarily unreachable by an ordinary attack. Apply the relevant move definition.

Terrain clauses specify whether they inspect the attacker or the recipient. For example, ordinary terrain type boosts inspect the grounded attacker, whereas Psychic Terrain's priority protection inspects the grounded target.
