# Critical hits

Critical-hit chances, damage multiplier, ignored stages, screens, and effects critical hits do not bypass.

## Reference text

The normal critical-hit chance is 1/24. One additional critical-hit stage raises it to 1/8; two additional stages raise it to 1/2; three or more make a critical hit certain unless an effect prevents it. A move or ability can explicitly guarantee or prevent critical hits.

A critical hit normally multiplies damage by 1.5. It ignores negative stages in the offensive statistic used for that hit and positive stages in the defensive statistic used for that hit. Favorable offensive boosts and unfavorable defensive drops still matter.

A critical hit bypasses Reflect, Light Screen, and Aurora Veil's damage reduction. It does not generally bypass type immunities, Protect, Substitute, or every ability-based reduction. It does not automatically remove the burn damage penalty. Effects such as Sniper can alter the critical-hit damage multiplier.
