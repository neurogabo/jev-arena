# Leech Seed, binding, Salt Cure, and Curse

Leech Seed, binding, Champions Salt Cure, and Curse: damage, source links, removal, and switching.

## Reference text

Include when an applicable persistent-damage effect exists or can be established. Dependencies: residual order, grounding and trapping as applicable, switching, Substitute, prevention abilities.

**Leech Seed:** At its residual event, the affected Pokémon normally loses 1/8 maximum HP and the Pokémon occupying the seeder's position receives the corresponding recovery, subject to damage and healing modifiers. Grass types are immune to its initial application. Ordinary switching by the seeded Pokémon removes it; the seeder's replacement can receive the healing. Baton Pass can carry the seeded effect. Rapid Spin or Mortal Spin can remove it under their own successful-use conditions.

**Binding moves:** Effects such as Wrap, Whirlpool, or Infestation normally restrict voluntary switching and cause 1/8 maximum-HP damage per applicable residual event for four or five turns. They normally end when the relevant source or target leaves. A binding effect is distinct from a trapping ability and from a move lock. Ghost-type trapping immunity and Champions Run Away can change the switch restriction without necessarily preventing the original hit or its damage-over-time effect. Substitute can remove the binding effect when successfully created. A repeated binding move does not ordinarily stack an independent second binding counter.

**Salt Cure — Champions:** The ongoing effect deals **1/16 maximum HP**, or **1/8** if the affected Pokémon currently has Water or Steel typing, at its residual event. Being both Water and Steel does not apply the enhanced rate twice. This is in addition to Salt Cure's initial attack. Ordinary switching removes the effect. Protect can block the initial eligible hit but does not stop residual damage from an already established effect.

**Curse:** Its effect depends on the user's current type. For a non-Ghost user, it lowers Speed one stage and raises Attack and Defense one stage. For a Ghost user, it normally sacrifices half the user's maximum HP and establishes a curse that removes one quarter of the target's maximum HP per residual event. The Ghost user's sacrifice can cause it to faint. Ordinary switching clears the target's curse, while Baton Pass can transfer it. Use the target and failure rules from the supplied move definition.
