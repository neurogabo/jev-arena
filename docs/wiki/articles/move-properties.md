# Move properties

Move type, category, power, accuracy, target, contact, tags, secondary effects, and self effects.

## Reference text

Keep these concepts distinct:

- **Type** determines ordinary type effectiveness and same-type attack bonus.
- **Category** is Physical, Special, or Status. It is independent of type.
- **Base power** is an input to damage, not the resulting HP loss.
- **Accuracy** describes an ordinary hit check. A move that bypasses that check can still fail against protection, an immunity, or an unreachable target.
- **Priority** places the move in an action-order bracket.
- **Contact** is a separate property. Not all physical moves make contact, and contact is not determined by damage category.
- **Spread targeting** determines the set of targets; it is not synonymous with multiple hits.
- **Secondary effects** are effects handled after the relevant primary hit, such as a chance to flinch or lower a statistic. Some moves have important primary effects instead.
- **Self effects** affect the user, such as recoil or a Special Attack drop, and can have different conditions from effects on the target.
- **Move tags** such as sound, powder, bullet, pulse, punch, slicing, bite, wind, and healing matter when a supplied ability or item refers to them.

A Showdown `protect` flag means that the move is normally blockable by protection. It does not mean the move protects its user. A `bypasssub` flag concerns Substitute, not Protect. Keep these meanings separate.
