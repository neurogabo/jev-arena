# Choice lock, charging, recharge, and first-turn moves

Choice lock, charging, recharge, first-action moves, entry history, and forced Struggle.

## Reference text

Include the relevant mechanics. Dependencies: PP, action history, switching, move definition.

Choice Scarf multiplies Speed by 1.5 while effective and normally locks the holder into the first move it executes. Ordinary switching clears the move lock. Temporary item suppression and changes to the held item can affect the lock's operation. A Choice item being familiar from another game does not make that item legal in this regulation; this entry uses Choice Scarf because it is present in the pinned Champions item data.

A two-turn move can spend one action charging or entering a semi-invulnerable state and perform its attack on the following action. The continuation, target, and escape rules are move-specific. Do not treat the second turn as an unrestricted fresh choice. Solar Beam under sun is an example of a condition that can remove a charging turn.

Recharge moves can force a following action to be spent recharging when their triggering conditions are met. A forced recharge is distinct from paralysis, and the request can already encode the forced action. Multi-turn rampage moves have their own locking duration and aftermath.

Fake Out and First Impression are available only for the qualifying first move action after entry. The Champions override disables them once that entry's action opportunity has been used; an earlier action prevented by a status does not necessarily preserve the opportunity. Ordinary switching out and back in resets the relevant entry state. Fake Out has priority +3 and a flinching secondary effect; First Impression has priority +2 and base power 100 in Champions.

If no ordinary known move can be selected, the engine can supply Struggle. Struggle deals typeless damage to a selected-by-engine opposing target and has its own quarter-maximum-HP recoil rule. It is not a new move that can be chosen freely when other moves remain available.
