# Other type-based immunities

Type-based status, powder, trapping, Prankster, and weather immunities beyond ordinary damage effectiveness.

## Reference text

Under the ordinary rules, Fire types cannot newly receive burn; Electric types cannot newly receive paralysis; Ice types cannot newly receive freeze; and Poison and Steel types cannot newly receive poison or bad poison. Corrosion is a specific exception to the ordinary poisoning type restriction when applicable.

Grass types are immune to powder effects. This includes the relevant check for whether an attacker follows Rage Powder's redirection. Grass types are also immune to the initial application of Leech Seed through that move's specific rule.

Ghost types are immune to ordinary trapping restrictions. This does not mean they can ignore every move lock, charging turn, or other limitation. Opposing Dark types are immune to applicable status moves boosted by Prankster; this is not immunity to all status moves or all priority attacks.

Rock, Ground, and Steel types are immune to ordinary sandstorm chip damage. Only Rock types receive sandstorm's Special Defense increase. Ice types receive snow's Defense increase. Snow does not deal the old hail chip damage.

Changing into an immune type does not by itself mean an existing major status is cured. Apply any explicit cure or removal effect separately.
