# Safeguard, healing, and healing prevention

Safeguard, Wish, Roost, weather-dependent healing, Pain Split, and healing prevention.

## Reference text

Include the subrules relevant to current or considered effects. Dependencies: major status, residual order, source/recipient identity, items and abilities.

Safeguard normally protects the user's side from opposing applications of major status and confusion for five turns. It does not cure an existing condition. Its Yawn rule prevents initial application but does not necessarily stop an already pending Yawn from causing sleep. Infiltrator and explicit bypass effects need their own handling.

Recovery cannot ordinarily raise HP above maximum HP. A healing move can fail at full HP or be prevented by a healing-block effect. Different recovery moves restore different fractions and may use different integer rounding; use their specific definitions at exact HP thresholds.

Wish establishes a delayed recovery attached to a position. At the end of the following turn, the occupant of that position can recover half the original user's maximum HP, rounded down. Switching the Wish user can therefore deliver the recovery to a reserve. The recipient's own maximum HP is not used to set Wish's healing amount.

Roost ordinarily restores half maximum HP and removes the user's Flying typing for the remainder of the turn if the healing succeeds. A pure Flying user temporarily becomes Normal under the move's rule. This can change a later Ground matchup and terrain exposure; it does not permanently change the species.

Synthesis, Morning Sun, and Moonlight ordinarily recover one half maximum HP without weather, two thirds in effective sun, and one quarter in rain, sandstorm, or snow, subject to their precise definitions.

Pain Split sets both Pokémon toward the rounded average of their current HP, capped separately by each Pokémon's maximum HP. It is not an ordinary damaging attack followed by an ordinary healing move.

Psychic Noise can impose a two-turn healing-block effect through its secondary-effect handling. That condition can prevent healing and draining moves and many ability/item recovery effects while the target remains active. Pain Split and Regenerator are exempt from this healing block.
