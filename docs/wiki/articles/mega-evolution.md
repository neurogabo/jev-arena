# Mega Evolution and current form

Mega eligibility, team resource, transformation timing, current form statistics and ability, and persistence.

## Reference text

Use the available Mega Evolution options supplied for the request. Only one Mega Evolution may be used by a side in a battle. Holding several compatible Mega Stones does not permit several Mega Evolutions.

A declaration is associated with the chosen action of an eligible active Pokémon. The transformation happens before ordinary moves and changes the Pokémon to its specified Mega form. Use that form's types, statistics, and ability for subsequent applicable events. The new Speed can affect the same turn's remaining action order.

Mega Evolution is not itself an extra damaging move. An entry-like effect from the new ability can occur during transformation. A Pokémon that already triggered a base-form ability on entry can therefore have a different ability after Mega Evolution.

Mega Evolution remains after switching out and fainting. The resulting form after Revival Blessing is uncertain. The Mega Stone is not consumed by the transformation.

The supplied form and eligibility fields determine the available transformation. A form whose name contains “Z” is not, for that reason, a Z-Move. Do not introduce Terastallization, Dynamax, or another generation's battle feature into this environment without an explicit supported action and rule.
