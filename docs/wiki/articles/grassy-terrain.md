# Grassy Terrain

Grounded Grass power, residual healing, selected Ground-move reduction, and Grassy Glide priority.

## Reference text

Dependencies: M16, grounding, residual healing, relevant move definitions.

Grass attacks by grounded Pokémon have their power multiplied by 1.3. Grounded active Pokémon recover 1/16 maximum HP at the terrain's recovery event, including the relevant final-turn recovery before the terrain expires.

Earthquake, Bulldoze, and Magnitude have their power halved against grounded targets while the terrain applies. This is not a blanket halving of every Ground move.

Grassy Glide gains one priority level when its user is grounded and Grassy Terrain applies. Use that resulting priority when considering action order and priority-blocking effects.
