# Abilities and held items

Ability suppression versus bypass, item presence versus consumption, and trigger-specific timing.

## Reference text

An ability can be known but currently ineffective because it is suppressed, bypassed for a particular interaction, or unavailable in the current form. These states are different. Neutralizing Gas suppresses applicable abilities while present; Mold Breaker-style effects bypass specified abilities during an applicable move without permanently deleting them.

Abilities can activate on entry, on a hit, when statistics change, at an HP threshold, on a knockout, on switching out, or during a residual phase. Merely naming the ability does not specify when it applies. Include its triggering condition and relevant exceptions.

A held item can be unknown, present and active, temporarily suppressed, consumed, removed, or replaced. An item that has been consumed does not activate again just because the Pokémon switches out. A suppression effect does not necessarily remove the item itself.

An item can be automatically consumed during another action; it does not require a separate chosen command. HP thresholds must be evaluated at the time specified by the item. Some items and abilities act between hits, while others act after an entire move.

Legality and item availability come from the selected Champions format. A familiar item from another VGC game must not be assumed available here. Current entity definitions and the request are the source of the Pokémon's item-related behavior.
