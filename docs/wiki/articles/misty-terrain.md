# Misty Terrain

Grounded status and confusion prevention, Dragon power reduction, and delayed Yawn sleep.

## Reference text

Dependencies: M16, grounding, major status, confusion and Yawn.

Grounded Pokémon are protected from newly inflicted major status and confusion. Existing statuses are not automatically cured. Grounded recipients take half the ordinary power from Dragon attacks under the terrain's rule, regardless of whether the Dragon attacker is grounded.

Misty Terrain does not provide a general Fairy attack boost. A grounded Pokémon can become drowsy from Yawn, but the terrain prevents the resulting sleep while its protection applies. If the protection disappears before the sleep event, the remaining Yawn conditions must be considered again.
