# Mega Evolution and current form state

`interaction:mega-form-state` · Starting ability, transformation eligibility, and current Mega ability are distinct.

## When to retrieve

A Pokémon holds a compatible Mega Stone or has already Mega Evolved.

## Conditional rule

A compatible stone enables the corresponding transformation when the side still has its permitted Mega Evolution. The resulting form has its own types, base statistics, and ability. Keep the starting set and current form separate. Champions keeps the Mega form after withdrawal and fainting.

## Exceptions and required state

Holding a stone does not prove transformation occurred. A team can carry several stones but use only one Mega Evolution per battle. A known Mega ability does not identify an unknown starting ability. The resulting form after Revival Blessing is uncertain.
