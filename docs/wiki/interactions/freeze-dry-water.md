# Freeze-Dry and Water typing

`interaction:freeze-dry-water` · A move-specific effectiveness override is not ordinary Ice coverage.

## When to retrieve

Freeze-Dry may hit a Water-type target.

## Conditional rule

Freeze-Dry sets its effectiveness against the Water component to super effective. Combine that factor with the target’s other current type component and remaining modifiers. The Champions move has no secondary freeze chance.

## Exceptions and required state

This is not a general rule for all Ice moves and does not remove independent accuracy, protection, or ability checks. If another effect changes the move’s type, account for its effective type and the resulting effectiveness rule.
