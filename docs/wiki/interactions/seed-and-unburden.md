# Terrain Seeds and Unburden

`interaction:seed-and-unburden` · Item consumption and terrain activation can change both bulk and Speed.

## When to retrieve

A terrain seed holder with Unburden may enter matching terrain or have matching terrain set.

## Conditional rule

An effective matching Terrain Seed consumes itself when its activation conditions are met and raises the corresponding defensive stat. Effective Unburden can double Speed after the held item is lost or consumed. This can change the remaining action order.

## Exceptions and required state

A Pokémon merely starting without an item does not automatically receive the boost. Check actual consumption, item suppression, stat limits, active ability, switch history, and whether a new item has been gained. The seed trigger itself does not require the holder to be grounded.
