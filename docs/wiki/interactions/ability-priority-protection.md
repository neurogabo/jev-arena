# Armor Tail and Queenly Majesty

`interaction:ability-priority-protection` · Active ability holders can protect their side from opposing priority.

## When to retrieve

One of these abilities is active and a positive-priority opposing move may target its side.

## Conditional rule

These abilities can block opposing positive-priority moves aimed at the holder or its allies. Unlike Psychic Terrain’s protection, the ability rule does not require the protected target to be grounded.

## Exceptions and required state

The holder must be active and the ability effective at that moment. Suppression and applicable ability-ignoring effects matter. It is not a general immunity to all moves or a guarantee against a move whose final priority is nonpositive.
