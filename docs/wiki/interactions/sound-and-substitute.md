# Sound moves and Substitute

`interaction:sound-and-substitute` · A bypass flag and sound immunity are different checks.

## When to retrieve

A contemplated sound move faces a Substitute or Soundproof holder.

## Conditional rule

Moves carrying the bypasssub flag can affect the Pokémon behind a Substitute instead of being absorbed by that substitute. Many sound moves carry this flag. Soundproof can separately prevent applicable sound moves from affecting its holder.

## Exceptions and required state

Inspect the actual resolved move flags; do not infer all exceptions from a name or animation. Bypassing Substitute does not bypass Soundproof, typing, accuracy, or protection automatically. Ability suppression or an applicable ability-ignoring move changes the Soundproof check.
