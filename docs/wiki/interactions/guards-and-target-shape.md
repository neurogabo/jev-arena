# Wide Guard, Quick Guard, and target shape

`interaction:guards-and-target-shape` · Side protection depends on spread targeting or positive priority.

## When to retrieve

A side may use Wide Guard or Quick Guard before an incoming move.

## Conditional rule

Wide Guard protects its side against eligible moves whose target is all adjacent Pokémon or all adjacent foes, including an ally’s spread move. Quick Guard protects its side from eligible positive-priority moves. Their repeat-use rules differ from ordinary Protect: they update the shared protection counter but do not use it as their own failure roll.

## Exceptions and required state

Use the move’s actual target/priority and protection eligibility, and check protection bypass. Wide Guard is not inferred solely from how many targets remain; a spread-target move can still qualify with only one target present.
