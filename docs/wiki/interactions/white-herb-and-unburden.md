# White Herb, stat drops, and Unburden

`interaction:white-herb-and-unburden` · A consumed White Herb can remove negative stages and activate Unburden.

## When to retrieve

A White Herb holder may acquire negative stat stages, especially when it has Unburden.

## Conditional rule

When its activation conditions are met, White Herb restores the holder’s negative stat stages to zero and is consumed. The resulting item loss can activate effective Unburden. Positive stages are not reset by this item.

## Exceptions and required state

Check that negative stages exist, the item can activate, and consumption actually occurs. Do not assume every intended stat drop succeeds or that a previously consumed Herb remains available. New item acquisition and switching affect Unburden state.
