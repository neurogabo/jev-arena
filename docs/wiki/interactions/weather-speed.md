# Weather abilities and Speed order

`interaction:weather-speed` · Weather-dependent Speed changes interact with Trick Room and suppression.

## When to retrieve

Rain, sun, sandstorm, or snow can activate a relevant Speed ability.

## Conditional rule

Swift Swim, Chlorophyll, Sand Rush, and Slush Rush double their holder’s Speed in their corresponding effective weather. Trick Room reverses the Speed comparison within the same priority bracket; a faster Pokémon can therefore act later under it.

## Exceptions and required state

Check active weather, weather suppression, effective ability, stat stages, and other Speed modifiers. Weather does not change move priority by this mechanism. Champions Trick Room has no older extreme-Speed wraparound.
