# Intimidate and stat-drop responses

`interaction:intimidate-and-stat-responses` · A switch-in Attack drop can fail or trigger a retaliatory boost.

## When to retrieve

An effective Intimidate user enters, or an opposing stat drop may reach a Defiant or Competitive holder.

## Conditional rule

Intimidate attempts to lower opposing Attack by one stage. Substitute and applicable stat-drop/Intimidate immunity can prevent the drop. When an opposing Pokémon actually lowers a stat stage, Defiant can raise Attack by two stages and Competitive can raise Special Attack by two stages.

## Exceptions and required state

Check current abilities, suppression, stat limits, substitute, and whether a drop actually occurred. Defiant/Competitive are not triggered simply by seeing the opponent’s species. Their triggers concern opposing-caused drops, not ordinary self-caused drops.
