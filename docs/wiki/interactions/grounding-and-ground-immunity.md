# Grounding and Ground-type immunity

`interaction:grounding-and-ground-immunity` · Flying, Levitate, and forced grounding require separate checks.

## When to retrieve

A Ground move, terrain effect, Gravity, or a grounding-changing effect may matter.

## Conditional rule

Flying typing normally supplies a type-chart Ground immunity. Effective Levitate supplies an ability-based Ground immunity. Gravity and other grounding effects can remove relevant airborne protections. Ordinary Ground attacks should use the target’s current grounding and the specific immunity source.

## Exceptions and required state

A Mold Breaker-style bypass can ignore an applicable ability immunity without removing Flying typing. Move-specific exceptions require their own definitions. Iron Ball, Air Balloon, Magnet Rise, and semi-invulnerability are not interchangeable flags.
