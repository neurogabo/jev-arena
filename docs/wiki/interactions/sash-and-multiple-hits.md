# Focus Sash and multiple hits

`interaction:sash-and-multiple-hits` · Full-HP survival is evaluated for the hit that would cause fainting.

## When to retrieve

Focus Sash is known or plausible, particularly against a multi-hit attack or residual damage.

## Conditional rule

An effective Focus Sash can let a full-HP holder survive a damaging attack’s otherwise fatal hit at 1 HP and is consumed. Later hits of a multi-hit move can still knock out the holder. Damage taken earlier can remove the full-HP condition.

## Exceptions and required state

This is not a universal guarantee to survive a turn. Track current HP, item presence/suppression, the order of hits, and damage that is not eligible for the item trigger. Do not invent exact HP from rounded opponent percentages.
