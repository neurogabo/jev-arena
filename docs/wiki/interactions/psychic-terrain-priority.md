# Psychic Terrain and opposing priority

`interaction:psychic-terrain-priority` · Grounded targets, positive priority, and side identity determine this protection.

## When to retrieve

Psychic Terrain is active or may be set, and a contemplated attack has positive effective priority.

## Conditional rule

Psychic Terrain blocks opposing positive-priority moves against grounded targets. Test the target’s grounding and the move’s effective priority when it would hit. The user’s own grounding is not the condition for this protection.

## Exceptions and required state

It does not block ally-targeted moves by this rule, nor protect an airborne target. This terrain protection does not apply to self-targeted moves or semi-invulnerable targets; those have separate hit checks. Abilities and other effects can change grounding or priority.
