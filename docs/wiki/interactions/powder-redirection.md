# Rage Powder, Follow Me, and powder immunity

`interaction:powder-redirection` · Redirection eligibility depends on move shape, actor immunity, and active effects.

## When to retrieve

An active Follow Me or Rage Powder user may attract a targeted move.

## Conditional rule

Follow Me and Rage Powder redirect eligible opposing single-target moves while their effect is active. Rage Powder is subject to powder immunity: a Grass-type attacker or an attacker with effective Overcoat ignores that powder-based redirection. Follow Me does not inherit that powder exception.

## Exceptions and required state

Spread moves are not converted into single-target moves by redirection. Check the move’s targeting and any redirection-ignoring ability or move rule. Safety Goggles is not admitted in this pinned Champions item catalogue and must not be assumed available.
