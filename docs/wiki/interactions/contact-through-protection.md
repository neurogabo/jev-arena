# Champions contact damage through protection

`interaction:contact-through-protection` · Unseen Fist and Piercing Drill can permit quarter damage through protection.

## When to retrieve

A contact move from a holder of effective Unseen Fist or Piercing Drill encounters protection.

## Conditional rule

The Champions protection hook marks eligible contact hits as bypassing protection. The damage engine then applies a 0.25 damage modifier for that bypass. Do not import the older assumption that Unseen Fist always deals full damage through Protect.

## Exceptions and required state

This requires an effective ability and contact flag at the relevant check. It does not grant universal success against immunities, accuracy failure, or every other defensive effect. Protection-specific side effects and hit processing still require their own rules.
