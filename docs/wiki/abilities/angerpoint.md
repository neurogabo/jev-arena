# Anger Point

`ability:angerpoint` · If this Pokemon (not its substitute) takes a critical hit, its Attack is raised 12 stages.

## Definition

If this Pokemon, but not its substitute, is struck by a critical hit, its Attack is raised by 12 stages.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
