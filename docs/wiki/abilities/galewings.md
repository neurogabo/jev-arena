# Gale Wings

`ability:galewings` · If this Pokemon is at full HP, its Flying-type moves have their priority increased by 1.

## Definition

If this Pokemon is at full HP, its Flying-type moves have their priority increased by 1.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
