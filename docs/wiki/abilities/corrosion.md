# Corrosion

`ability:corrosion` · This Pokemon can poison or badly poison a Pokemon regardless of its typing.

## Definition

This Pokemon can poison or badly poison a Pokemon regardless of its typing.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
