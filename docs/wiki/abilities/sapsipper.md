# Sap Sipper

`ability:sapsipper` · This Pokemon's Attack is raised 1 stage if hit by a Grass move; Grass immunity.

## Definition

This Pokemon is immune to Grass-type moves and raises its Attack by 1 stage when hit by a Grass-type move.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
