# Poison Touch

`ability:poisontouch` · This Pokemon's contact moves have a 30% chance of poisoning.

## Definition

This Pokemon's contact moves have a 30% chance of poisoning. This effect comes after a move's inherent secondary effect chance.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
