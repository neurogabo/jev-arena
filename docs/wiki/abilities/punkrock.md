# Punk Rock

`ability:punkrock` · This Pokemon receives 1/2 damage from sound moves. Its own have 1.3× power.

## Definition

This Pokemon's sound-based moves have their power multiplied by 1.3. This Pokemon takes halved damage from sound-based moves.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
