# Reckless

`ability:reckless` · This Pokemon's attacks with recoil or crash damage have 1.2× power; not Struggle.

## Definition

This Pokemon's attacks with recoil or crash damage have their power multiplied by 1.2. Does not affect Struggle.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
