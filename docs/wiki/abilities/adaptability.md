# Adaptability

`ability:adaptability` · This Pokemon's same-type attack bonus (STAB) is 2 instead of 1.5.

## Definition

This Pokemon's moves that match one of its types have a same-type attack bonus (STAB) of 2 instead of 1.5.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
