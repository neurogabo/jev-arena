# Quick Draw

`ability:quickdraw` · This Pokemon has a 30% chance to move first in its priority bracket with attacking moves.

## Definition

This Pokemon has a 30% chance to move first in its priority bracket with attacking moves.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
