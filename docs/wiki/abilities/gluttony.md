# Gluttony

`ability:gluttony` · This Pokemon eats Berries at 1/2 max HP or less instead of their usual 1/4 max HP.

## Definition

When this Pokemon is holding a Berry that usually activates with 1/4 or less of its maximum HP, it is eaten at 1/2 or less of its maximum HP instead.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
