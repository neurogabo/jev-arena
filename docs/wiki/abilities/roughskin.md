# Rough Skin

`ability:roughskin` · Pokemon making contact with this Pokemon lose 1/8 of their max HP.

## Definition

Pokemon making contact with this Pokemon lose 1/8 of their maximum HP, rounded down.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
