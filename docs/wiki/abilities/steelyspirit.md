# Steely Spirit

`ability:steelyspirit` · This Pokemon and its allies' Steel-type moves have their power multiplied by 1.5.

## Definition

This Pokemon and its allies' Steel-type moves have their power multiplied by 1.5. This affects Doom Desire even if the user is not on the field.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
