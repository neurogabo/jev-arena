# Cloud Nine

`ability:cloudnine` · While this Pokemon is active, the effects of weather conditions are disabled.

## Definition

While this Pokemon is active, the effects of weather conditions are disabled.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
