# Berserk

`ability:berserk` · This Pokemon's Sp. Atk is raised by 1 when it reaches 1/2 or less of its max HP.

## Definition

When this Pokemon has more than 1/2 its maximum HP and takes damage from an attack bringing it to 1/2 or less of its maximum HP, its Special Attack is raised by 1 stage. This effect is prevented if the move had a secondary effect removed by the Sheer Force Ability.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.

Uncertain interaction: exact HP-threshold timing during multi-hit attacks.
