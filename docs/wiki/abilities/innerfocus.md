# Inner Focus

`ability:innerfocus` · This Pokemon cannot be made to flinch. Immune to Intimidate.

## Definition

This Pokemon cannot be made to flinch. This Pokemon is immune to the effect of the Intimidate Ability.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
