# Disguise

`ability:disguise` · (Mimikyu only) The first hit it takes is blocked, and it takes 1/8 HP damage instead.

## Definition

If this Pokemon is a Mimikyu, the first hit it takes in battle deals 0 neutral damage. Its disguise is then broken, it changes to Busted Form, and it loses 1/8 of its max HP. Confusion damage also breaks the disguise.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
