# Intimidate

`ability:intimidate` · On switch-in, this Pokemon lowers the Attack of opponents by 1 stage.

## Definition

On switch-in, this Pokemon lowers the Attack of opposing Pokemon by 1 stage. Pokemon with the Inner Focus, Oblivious, Own Tempo, or Scrappy Abilities and Pokemon behind a substitute are immune.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
