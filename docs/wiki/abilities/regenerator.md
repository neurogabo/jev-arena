# Regenerator

`ability:regenerator` · This Pokemon restores 1/3 of its maximum HP, rounded down, when it switches out.

## Definition

This Pokemon restores 1/3 of its maximum HP, rounded down, when it switches out.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
