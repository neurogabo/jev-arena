# Justified

`ability:justified` · This Pokemon's Attack is raised by 1 stage after it is damaged by a Dark-type move.

## Definition

This Pokemon's Attack is raised by 1 stage after it is damaged by a Dark-type move.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
