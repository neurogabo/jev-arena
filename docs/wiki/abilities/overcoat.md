# Overcoat

`ability:overcoat` · This Pokemon is immune to powder moves, Sandstorm damage, and Effect Spore.

## Definition

This Pokemon is immune to powder moves, damage from Sandstorm, and the effects of Rage Powder and the Effect Spore Ability.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
