# Aroma Veil

`ability:aromaveil` · Protects user/allies from Attract, Disable, Encore, Heal Block, Taunt, and Torment.

## Definition

This Pokemon and its allies cannot become affected by Attract, Disable, Encore, Heal Block, Taunt, or Torment.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
