# Hospitality

`ability:hospitality` · On switch-in, this Pokemon restores 1/4 of its ally's maximum HP, rounded down.

## Definition

On switch-in, this Pokemon restores 1/4 of its ally's maximum HP, rounded down.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
