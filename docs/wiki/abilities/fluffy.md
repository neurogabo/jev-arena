# Fluffy

`ability:fluffy` · This Pokemon takes 1/2 damage from contact moves, 2× damage from Fire moves.

## Definition

This Pokemon receives 1/2 damage from contact moves, but double damage from Fire moves.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
