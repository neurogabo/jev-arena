# Super Luck

`ability:superluck` · This Pokemon's critical hit ratio is raised by 1 stage.

## Definition

This Pokemon's critical hit ratio is raised by 1 stage.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
