# Torrent

`ability:torrent` · At 1/3 or less of its max HP, this Pokemon's offensive stat is 1.5× with Water attacks.

## Definition

When this Pokemon has 1/3 or less of its maximum HP, rounded down, its offensive stat is multiplied by 1.5 while using a Water-type attack.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
