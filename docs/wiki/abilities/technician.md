# Technician

`ability:technician` · This Pokemon's moves of 60 power or less have 1.5× power, including Struggle.

## Definition

This Pokemon's moves of 60 power or less have their power multiplied by 1.5, including Struggle. This effect comes after a move's effect changes its own power.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
