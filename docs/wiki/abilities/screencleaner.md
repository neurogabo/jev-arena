# Screen Cleaner

`ability:screencleaner` · On switch-in, the effects of Aurora Veil, Light Screen, and Reflect end for both sides.

## Definition

On switch-in, the effects of Aurora Veil, Light Screen, and Reflect end for both sides.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
