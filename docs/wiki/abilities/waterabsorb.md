# Water Absorb

`ability:waterabsorb` · This Pokemon heals 1/4 of its max HP when hit by Water moves; Water immunity.

## Definition

This Pokemon is immune to Water-type moves and restores 1/4 of its maximum HP, rounded down, when hit by a Water-type move.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
