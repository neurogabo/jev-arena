# Sticky Hold

`ability:stickyhold` · This Pokemon cannot lose its held item due to another Pokemon's Ability or attack.

## Definition

This Pokemon cannot lose its held item due to another Pokemon's Ability or attack, unless the attack knocks out this Pokemon. A Sticky Barb will be transferred to other Pokemon regardless of this Ability.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
