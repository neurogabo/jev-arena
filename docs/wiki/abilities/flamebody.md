# Flame Body

`ability:flamebody` · 30% chance a Pokemon making contact with this Pokemon will be burned.

## Definition

30% chance a Pokemon making contact with this Pokemon will be burned.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
