# Motor Drive

`ability:motordrive` · This Pokemon's Speed is raised 1 stage if hit by an Electric move; Electric immunity.

## Definition

This Pokemon is immune to Electric-type moves and raises its Speed by 1 stage when hit by an Electric-type move.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
