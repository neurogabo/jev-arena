# Gooey

`ability:gooey` · Pokemon making contact with this Pokemon have their Speed lowered by 1 stage.

## Definition

Pokemon making contact with this Pokemon have their Speed lowered by 1 stage.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
