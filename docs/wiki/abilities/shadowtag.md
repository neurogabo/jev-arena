# Shadow Tag

`ability:shadowtag` · Prevents foes from choosing to switch unless they also have this Ability.

## Definition

Prevents opposing Pokemon from choosing to switch out, unless they are holding a Shed Shell, are a Ghost type, or also have this Ability.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
