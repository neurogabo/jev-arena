# Unaware

`ability:unaware` · This Pokemon ignores other Pokemon's stat stages when taking or doing damage.

## Definition

This Pokemon ignores other Pokemon's Attack, Special Attack, and accuracy stat stages when taking damage, and ignores other Pokemon's Defense, Special Defense, and evasiveness stat stages when dealing damage.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
