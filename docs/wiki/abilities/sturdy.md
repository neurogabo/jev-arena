# Sturdy

`ability:sturdy` · If this Pokemon is at full HP, it survives one hit with at least 1 HP. Immune to OHKO.

## Definition

If this Pokemon is at full HP, it survives one hit with at least 1 HP. OHKO moves fail when used against this Pokemon.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
