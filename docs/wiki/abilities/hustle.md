# Hustle

`ability:hustle` · This Pokemon's Attack is 1.5× and accuracy of its physical attacks is 0.8×.

## Definition

This Pokemon's Attack is multiplied by 1.5 and the accuracy of its physical attacks is multiplied by 0.8.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
