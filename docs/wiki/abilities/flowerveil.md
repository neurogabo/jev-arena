# Flower Veil

`ability:flowerveil` · This side's Grass types can't have stats lowered or status inflicted by other Pokemon.

## Definition

Grass-type Pokemon on this Pokemon's side cannot have their stat stages lowered by other Pokemon or have a non-volatile status condition inflicted on them by other Pokemon.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
