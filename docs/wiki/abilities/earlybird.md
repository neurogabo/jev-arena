# Early Bird

`ability:earlybird` · This Pokemon's sleep counter drops by 2 instead of 1.

## Definition

This Pokemon's sleep counter drops by 2 instead of 1.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
