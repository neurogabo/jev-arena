# Effect Spore

`ability:effectspore` · 30% chance of poison/paralysis/sleep on others making contact with this Pokemon.

## Definition

30% chance a Pokemon making contact with this Pokemon will be poisoned, paralyzed, or fall asleep.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
