# Guard Dog

`ability:guarddog` · Immune to Intimidate. Intimidated: +1 Attack. Cannot be forced to switch out.

## Definition

This Pokemon is immune to the effect of the Intimidate Ability and raises its Attack by 1 stage instead. This Pokemon cannot be forced to switch out by another Pokemon's attack or item.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
