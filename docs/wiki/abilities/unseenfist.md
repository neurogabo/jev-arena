# Unseen Fist

`ability:unseenfist` · This Pokemon's contact moves ignore a target's protection and deal 1/4 the usual damage.

## Definition

This Pokemon's contact moves ignore a target's protection and deal 1/4 the usual damage.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
