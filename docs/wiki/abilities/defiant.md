# Defiant

`ability:defiant` · This Pokemon's Attack is raised by 2 for each of its stats that is lowered by a foe.

## Definition

This Pokemon's Attack is raised by 2 stages for each of its stat stages that is lowered by an opposing Pokemon.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
