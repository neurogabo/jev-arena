# Cute Charm

`ability:cutecharm` · 30% chance of infatuating Pokemon of the opposite gender if they make contact.

## Definition

There is a 30% chance a Pokemon making contact with this Pokemon will become infatuated if it is of the opposite gender.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
