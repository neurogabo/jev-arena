# Pixilate

`ability:pixilate` · This Pokemon's Normal-type moves become Fairy type and have 1.2× power.

## Definition

This Pokemon's Normal-type moves become Fairy-type moves and have their power multiplied by 1.2. This effect comes after other effects that change a move's type, but before Ion Deluge and Electrify's effects.

Apply this to the holder’s **current** ability. Its starting ability, Mega ability, copied ability, and suppression state can differ.
