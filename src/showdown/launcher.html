      How to play

      1. Pick a team, or choose Random team.
      2. Bring four Pokémon. The first two start on the field.
      3. Choose a move or a switch for each active Pokémon. When a move needs a target, choose who receives it.
      4. Win by knocking out all four of Jev’s Pokémon.

      A switch replaces a Pokémon on the field with one of your reserves. Mega Evolution is available once per battle when your Pokémon has the right stone.
